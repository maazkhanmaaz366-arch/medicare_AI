# MediCare AI Clinic Policies & Patient Guidelines

## Fasting Blood Glucose & Lipid Panel Prep
Patients scheduled for fasting blood tests (blood glucose, lipid profile, metabolic panel) must fast for at least 8 to 12 hours before the appointment. Plain water is permitted and encouraged to stay hydrated. Black coffee, tea, juices, gum, and mints are strictly prohibited until blood is drawn. Take routine prescribed blood pressure medications with a small sip of water unless otherwise directed by your physician.

## Appointment Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy
Appointments must be cancelled or rescheduled at least 24 hours in advance to avoid a $35 late cancellation fee. Routine general consultations can be cancelled directly through the clinic chat portal. However, surgical procedures and invasive diagnostic tests require medical staff and attending physician approval prior to cancellation to ensure patient safety and continuity of care.

## Insurance Acceptance & Payment Methods
MediCare AI Clinic accepts Medicare Part B, Medicaid, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, Cigna, and UnitedHealthcare. Co-pays are due at the time of check-in via credit card, debit card, HSA/FSA cards, or cash. Uninsured self-pay patients qualify for our 20% prompt-pay sliding scale discount upon registration.

## Cardiology Stress Test & ECG Preparation
For treadmill stress tests and ECG appointments: Wear comfortable loose-fitting athletic clothing and running or walking shoes. Do not consume caffeine, decaffeinated coffee, chocolate, or energy drinks for 24 hours prior to the test. Consult Dr. Emily Watson regarding whether to withhold beta-blocker medications on the morning of the exam.

## Dermatology Skin Biopsy & Full Body Check Prep
For full-body skin cancer checks: Remove all makeup, facial moisturizers, and nail polish so the dermatologist can examine skin lesions and nail beds thoroughly. Inform Dr. Sarah Lin if you are currently taking anticoagulant blood thinners such as Aspirin, Warfarin, or Eliquis prior to any minor skin excision or biopsy.

## Pediatrics Vaccination & Well-Child Exam Policy
Parents or legal guardians must accompany all minors under 18 years old for wellness checkups and childhood vaccinations. Please bring the child's official immunization record card. If the child has an active fever over 101°F (38.3°C) on the day of the visit, routine vaccinations will be rescheduled after the fever resolves.
